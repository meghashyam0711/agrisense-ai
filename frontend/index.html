TNAU Split Application Plan for Paddy

Basal Application (At Transplanting) Stage 1

Apply 25% Nitrogen, 100% Phosphorus (DAP/Superphosphate), and 50% Potassium (MOP). Incorporate into puddle soil.

Active Tillering (20-25 DAT) Stage 2 • Active Now

Top-dress 50% Nitrogen (Urea) when soil has thin water film. Ensure weeds are cleared before application.

Panicle Initiation (40-45 DAT) Stage 3

Apply remaining 25% Nitrogen and 50% Potassium to boost grain filling and reduce empty spikelets.

Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) Water Saving Method

Install a perforated field water pipe (15cm diameter). Allow field water to drop 15cm below the soil surface before re-irrigating up to 5cm depth. Conserves up to 30% water without reducing yield.
